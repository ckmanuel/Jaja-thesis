## STATEMENT OF THE PROBLEM

This study examines how mental health advocacy pages on Instagram employ lexical and grammatical strategies to construct hope and empowerment in captions, and how public comments respond to those strategies. Specifically, it aims to answer the following questions:

1. What are the lexical and grammatical features on Instagram captions construct hope and empowerment?

2. What linguistic features characterize public comment responses to hope- and empowerment-oriented captions in Instagram mental health advocacy posts?

3. What do the identified caption and comment patterns, taken together, reveal about how positive mental health meanings are discursively constructed in Instagram advocacy spaces?
