# Thesis formatting rules (binding)

Apply on every pass of `thesis/Thesis_Instagram_MH_*.docx`. Title page is exempt from spacing/line-spacing resets.

## Font and line spacing

- **Font:** Georgia everywhere (ascii / hAnsi / eastAsia / cs).
- **Line spacing:** 2.0 (double) on all content paragraphs and table cells.
- **Title page:** do not change spacing, line spacing, or indents.

## Bold (intentional only — no spills)

Keep bold only on:

- Section headings (major and bold minor)
- Table numbers (captions)
- Significance stakeholder labels (e.g. `Health Communication Researchers. `)
- Process step labels (e.g. `1. Corpus Screening. `)
- Definition of Terms term names only (e.g. `Hope Construction. `) — not the definition text
- Table header row cells

Everything else is regular weight.

## Major headers (exactly four)

- INTRODUCTION  
- METHODOLOGY  
- REFERENCES  
- APPENDICES  

Format:

- Georgia 14 bold  
- 3 spaces before, 3 spaces after (one space = 12 pt → 36 pt before/after)  
- No first-line indent  

## Minor headers

Two kinds:

1. **Bold subsections** — structural ALL CAPS headings that are not the four majors (e.g. BACKGROUND OF THE STUDY, STATEMENT OF THE PROBLEM, RESEARCH FRAMEWORK, SIGNIFICANCE OF THE STUDY, SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS, DEFINITION OF TERMS, RESEARCH CONTEXT / DESIGN / PROCESS / PARTICIPANTS, APPENDIX A / B).
2. **Italic themed sections** — BOTS subsection titles and Scope / Limitations / Delimitations.

Format (both kinds):

- Georgia 12 (bold or italic as above)  
- 1 space before (12 pt), **except 0 if the previous non-empty paragraph is a major header**  
- 0 space after  
- No first-line indent  

Do not insert blank spacer paragraphs around themed (italic) sections; spacing comes only from paragraph properties.

## Body

- Georgia 11  
- First-line indent 0.5″  
- 0 space before / after between body paragraphs  
- After a table: 1 space (12 pt) before the next non-major paragraph  

## References list

- Georgia 11  
- Hanging indent 0.5″ (left 0.5″, first-line −0.5″)  
- 0 space before / after  
- Double line spacing  

## Tables

- **Cell text:** Georgia 9; table horizontally centered on the page  
- **Borders (open-sided, no vertical lines):**  
  - Header row: thick top (sz 18) + thin bottom (sz 4)  
  - Last row: thick bottom (sz 18)  
  - Middle rows: no cell borders  
  - No full grid; no left/right borders; no tblBorders grid  
- **Caption:** Georgia 11 bold, **left-aligned** (not centered)  
- Table title line under caption (if any): Georgia 11, left-aligned  

## Empty paragraphs

After the title page, empty paragraphs must have 0 space before/after and must not be used to manufacture gaps. Prefer paragraph space_before / space_after on real headings.
